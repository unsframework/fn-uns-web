01 / THE IDEA

Connect once.
Build from there.

A Unified Namespace is a shared, structured data layer for your factory. Machines publish what happens. Other systems subscribe to what they need.

Understand the UNS →
01

Bring your signals together.

Collect machine states, counts, and measurements from equipment. Start with the signals needed to answer one useful question.

02

Give every signal a place.

Agree where each signal belongs and what it means, from the site down to the machine. Give every team the same names and context.

03

Let your systems share it.

Use that data in dashboards, production reports, and analysis. Add new uses without asking people to assemble the same exports again.
