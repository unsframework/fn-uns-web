01 / THE IDEA

Connect once.
Build from there.

A Unified Namespace is a shared, structured data layer for your factory. Machines publish what happens. Other systems subscribe to what they need.

Understand the UNS →
01

Bring your signals together.

Connect equipment to MQTT through producers and adapters. Keep machine states, counts, and measurements flowing.

02

Give every signal a place.

Organize data by site, area, line, cell, and equipment. Define the hierarchy and payload conventions in YAML.

03

Let your systems share it.

Build history, KPIs, dashboards, and AI analysis on the same context. Add subscribers as your needs grow.
